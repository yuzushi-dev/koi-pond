# Desktop demo: text alternative

The silent, 12-second recording shows Koi Pond running on a real Cinnamon X11
desktop across two monitors. The external display appears on the left and the
laptop display on the right. Their different heights leave a black strip below
the left display.

Orange, white and grey koi made from ASCII characters swim on a dark background.
The left display keeps its desktop icons, folders and bottom panel visible above
the animation. The right display shows the animated pond without desktop icons.
No applications, terminals, dialogs or browser windows are shown.

The wallpaper and MP4 recording use the configured 16 fps setting. The looping
GIF preview is sampled at 8 fps. This clip shows the animated desktop; it does
not demonstrate feeding, automatic pausing, login or monitor reconnection.

There is no speech or audio track. For a non-animated view, open the
[still preview](koi-pond-desktop.jpg). The [MP4](koi-pond-desktop.mp4) provides
playback controls in a video player.
